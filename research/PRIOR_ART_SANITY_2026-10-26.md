# Prior-art sanity check — 2026-10-26

**Status:** targeted manual/public-source search, **not** an exhaustive novelty,
patent, reproducibility, or clinical evaluation. No discovery is approved.

BioReason's new Crossref scout covers perovskite photodetectors,
neuromorphic computing, sodium-ion batteries and microfluidic biosensors.
Two superficially attractive cross-field questions already have substantial
nearby precedent, so generic versions must **not** be described as original.

## Candidate intersection: perovskite optical detection + neuromorphic computing

- [Zi et al., *Communications Materials* (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43246-026-01097-x),
  DOI **10.1038/s43246-026-01097-x**, reports perovskite-based all-photonic
  synapses for neuromorphic computing.
- [Chen et al., *Journal of Materials Chemistry C* (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4TC05007J),
  DOI **10.1039/D4TC05007J**, studies organic–inorganic perovskite
  optoelectronic synapses and reservoir computing.
- [Ehrler et al., *ACS Applied Electronic Materials* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaelm.5c02469),
  DOI **10.1021/acsaelm.5c02469**, covers microscale halide-perovskite
  optoelectronic synapses with switchable photocurrent.

**Decision:** a generic "use perovskite photodetectors for neuromorphic
computing" hypothesis has clear predecessors. Hold any claim of originality;
look for a narrowly specified gap, distinct metric, or experimental
constraint with a direct comparison before proposing another project.

## Candidate intersection: sodium-ion cells + optical sensing

- [*Light: Science & Applications* (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41377-026-02388-1),
  DOI **10.1038/s41377-026-02388-1**, describes operando multiresonant
  fiber-optic grating sensors tracking ion kinetics and state of charge in
  sodium-ion batteries.

**Decision:** optical monitoring of sodium-ion batteries is not a newly
unexplored field merely because BioReason found separate energy and sensing
papers. Any new hypothesis requires a more specific unanswered question and
study-by-study comparison.

## Experimental guidance to the council

1. Keep these studies as **near precedents**, not positive confirmations of
   a new cross-field mechanism.
2. A question is not automatically novel when it cites two different DOIs.
3. For each selected research lead, collect closest earlier studies, then
   state the exact claim, proposed improvement, measurable outcome and
   evidence needed to falsify it.
4. Do not assert exhaustive patent clearance or the absence of all analogous
   work. Papers and metadata are insufficient for that conclusion.

**Known limitation:** this note covers only two tempting intersections and a
few peer-reviewed articles. It is not a general search of GitHub, products,
preprints or patent databases, and is not embedded as a comprehensive
automated novelty gate.
