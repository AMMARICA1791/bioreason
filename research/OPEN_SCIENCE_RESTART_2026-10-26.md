## Actual next acceptance test

A genuine research improvement is **not** demonstrated until the council
outputs a falsifiable, nontrivial question grounded in at least one real
relevant paper, with a clearly named measured outcome and comparator;
the Critic identifies a specific uncertainty or possible falsifier;
the Inventor either repairs the question **without dropping evidence**
or explicitly explains why it should be withdrawn; and nearby research
and products are checked before any originality claim.
