# Frozen local-model capability diagnostic — protocol

## Purpose

Determine whether BioReason's current Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct model is able
to **retain a real source ID while revising a falsifiable question** after
explicit source-based criticism, or whether a modestly larger local
instruction-tuned model appears better at that *limited task*.

This is **not** a scientific reasoning benchmark and **not** a test that
eight collaborating agents outperform a solo model.

## Predefined comparison (written before viewing diagnostic results)

- Two fixed, bibliographically grounded excerpts from
  [a verified Europe PMC retrieval](https://github.com/AMMARICA1791/bioreason/actions/runs/40322483589):
  cervical-cancer Pap-smear AI screening (ID `42380812`) and automated MRI
  segmentation (ID `42802541`).
- The models are `Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct` and
  `Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`.
- Both receive the **same paper excerpts, role instruction, deterministic
  decoding**, and one follow-up generated by the **same rule-based critic**.
- Two model generations per case (initial and revision), four per model,
  **eight generations in total**, with no paid model APIs, model-weight
  training or autonomous internet navigation by the agents.
- For each model and case, archive initial and revised text, exact source-ID
  retention/loss, an identical 0–6 evidence-and-format proxy score, the
  independent citation/topic screening result, abstentions, and negative
  outcomes.
- Do not rerun until results look better or silently discard unfavourable
  cases. Archive both models' failures when they occur.

## Decision criteria

The larger model is a **credible candidate** for later targeted testing
only if it produces a properly anchored, non-placeholder tentative question
in at least one case, and its total source-retention and proxy performance
exceeds that of the 0.5B model. A two-case advantage is **not proof**
of better scientific reasoning, and must **not** automatically change the
eight-role council.

If both models are weak, the preferred engineering next step is to improve
source/proposal structure and verification, **not** add further agents or
pretend that longer deliberation equals learning.

An experimental improvement in proxy form is never independent scientific
validation, clinical advice, a patent claim, or proof of generalization.

- Inputs: [frozen cases](evaluation/model_capability_cases.json)
- Diagnostic: [source](scripts/model_capability_probe.py)

The ordinary BioReason council and its existing corrective memory are
**not changed** by this diagnostic.
